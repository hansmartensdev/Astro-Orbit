I'm Hans Martens, an Astro theme developer from Veghel, the Netherlands. In this post I tell you a bit more about myself.

## Computers since 1992

My interest in computers started long before I built websites. In 1992 I bought a new computer, and I've been interested in both the operating system and the hardware ever since. In 1998 I built my first computer from parts.

## Repairing laptops at the TU/e

In 2000 I started working as a computer service engineer, repairing laptops. I was seconded to the [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://www.tue.nl/), where students bought their laptops through the university. A new model arrived every academic year, so I worked through a long line of them: opening them up, finding what had failed, repairing it and putting them back together. It was a good job, and I liked working at the TU/e very much. I did several jobs after that, but this was the one I liked most.

## From WordPress to Astro

In 2010 I built my first website with [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/), for myself. I liked doing it very much, so I kept going, and I've never stopped building websites. In 2022 I moved to [Astro](https://astro.build/) and stayed. Astro ships zero JavaScript by default, gives me full control over the output and lets me add interactivity only where it's needed. Built this way, a fast site is the normal result.

## What I'm doing now

These days I work with Astro every day. I build websites, and I'm building a free, MIT-licensed Astro theme for the Astro community. I love building with AI: I use Claude Code to build high-quality websites.

## Working with AI

I'm very interested in working with AI, and in how it's evolving. I use [Claude Code](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-code), running on Opus 5.5. It's the best colleague I could have to build with, and it's also very good at advising me on the decisions I have to make in my work. For me, Claude Code is the best AI to work with, and it shows what AI is capable of these days.

## Hardware and operating systems today

I have a strong interest in computer hardware and operating systems. I often use [Ubuntu Linux](https://ubuntu.com/) as the operating system on my dual-boot laptop, and I'm interested in the [Raspberry Pi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/). I also love Apple products: my phone of choice is an iPhone, and the MacBook is a laptop that's nice to work with.

## Beyond the screen

I'm not in front of a monitor all day. Away from the keyboard you'll find me at the gym, enjoying good food and a proper espresso, in a good conversation, or out with my two dogs, who have strong opinions about how much time I should spend outside, and are usually right.
