Posts and projects are files in `src/content/`. Each file is one post or one project, with its settings at the top.

## Posts

Posts go in the folder of the site's language, `src/content/blog/en/` for an English site, as Markdown (`.md`) or MDX (`.mdx`). The top of a post looks like this:

```yaml
---
title: "Use Your Own Brand Colour"
description: "One setting changes the colour of the whole theme."
publishedAt: 2026-11-24T10:00:00Z
author: "Alex Morgan"
category: "Themes"
tags: ["Astro Orbit", "Colour"]
icon: "palette"
cardWord: "your own colour"
---
```

| Field | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| `title`, `description` | The title and the line under it, on the post and on its card. |
| `publishedAt` | The date. The newest posts come first. |
| `category` | `Astro`, `Themes` or `Performance`: the filters on the blog page. |
| `tags` | Words that link related posts: the posts under a post share its tags. Without an `icon`, one is picked from them. |
| `icon` | The icon on the card picture: any name from [lucide.dev/icons](https://lucide.dev/icons), or `astro` or `claude` for those logos. |
| `cardWord` | A word or a short phrase, up to about 20 characters, under the icon. |
| `featured` | The homepage shows four posts: the featured ones first, then the newest. |
| `pinned` | `true` keeps a post first on the homepage, whatever its date. |
| `heroImage`, `heroImageAlt` | A photo for the post's hero only. Its cards and its share image keep the card picture. |
| `draft` | `true` keeps the post off the site. |
| `toc` | `false` hides the table of contents. |

The card picture is made when the site is built, from the icon, the phrase and the brand colour, so a post doesn't need an image of its own. Each post also gets a reading time, a share image, a place in the RSS feed and in the site's search.

## Projects

Projects go in `src/content/work/`, one file each.

| Field | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| `title`, `discipline`, `description` | The name, the small label on the card, and one line about the project. |
| `client` | Shown in the facts under the project's hero. |
| `feature` | The card picture. `featureMobile` is the version for phones. |
| `pageHero`, `pageHeroMobile` | The picture at the top of the project page, for desktop and for phones. |
| `video` | One or more short screen recordings, shown beside the first section. |
| `challenge`, `solution` | The text of the first and the second section. |
| `gallery` | More pictures. `band: 1` or `band: 2` puts a picture beside the first or the second section. |
| `sections` | More sections after the first two, each with a label and text. |
| `order` | Lower comes first. The homepage shows the first six. |
| `nextLayout` | How the next project is shown at the foot of the page: `split` (the default) or `cinema`. |
| `draft` | `true` keeps the project off the site. |

A project without a picture stays out of the sitemap, and search engines don't index it until it has one.
