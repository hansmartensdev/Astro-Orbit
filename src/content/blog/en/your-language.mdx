Astro Orbit comes in English, Spanish, French and Dutch. The language decides the theme's own words: the menu, the buttons, the contact form's and the newsletter's messages, the confirmation email, the labels on a project page and the privacy page. It also decides how dates are written: "November 26, 2026" in English, "26 de noviembre de 2026" in Spanish, "26 novembre 2026" in French, "26 november 2026" in Dutch.

## Pick the language

The setting is in `src/config/theme.config.ts`:

```ts
export const language = 'es';
```

Your own text, in `theme.config.ts`, your posts and your projects, you write in that language yourself.

## Your posts

Posts go in a folder named after the language: `src/content/blog/es/` for a Spanish site. The folder doesn't appear in the address, so `src/content/blog/es/hola.mdx` is `/blog/hola/`. If the folder for your language is empty while another one has posts, the build says so.

## Another language

For a language the theme doesn't have yet, such as Chinese, with the code `zh`:

1. Copy `src/i18n/en.ts` to `src/i18n/zh.ts`, and translate the text between the quotes. `pnpm test` fails when a line is missing, so nothing stays in English by accident.
2. Copy `src/i18n/privacy/en.astro` to `src/i18n/privacy/zh.astro`, and translate the privacy text. Until it's there, the privacy page stays in English, and the build says so.
3. Set `language = 'zh'` in `theme.config.ts`.

Dates are written with the language data built into Node and the browsers, so a Chinese site shows "2026年11月26日" without a rule from you. Chinese and Japanese don't put spaces between words, so the reading time on a post counts their characters instead of their words.

## The share images

The share images and the blog covers are drawn when the site is built, with the theme's fonts, Outfit and Archivo, which have the Latin alphabet. English, Spanish, French and Dutch all fit. For a language in another script, such as Chinese, they can't draw the titles' letters. The pages themselves don't have that problem: the browser shows the letters with a font of its own.
