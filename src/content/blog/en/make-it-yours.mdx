The demo is filled in for a fictive designer, Alex Morgan. Two files make the site yours, and the components read from them, so you don't need to open the components to change a name or a line of text.

## Who you are: site.config.ts

`src/config/site.config.ts` holds:

- your name, and a sentence about you for search engines;
- your wordmark, `am/` in the demo. On hover it unfolds into your name when each letter is the first letter of a word of it;
- your email, where you are, and your social profiles;
- a WhatsApp link (`https://wa.me/<your number>`). With one, a WhatsApp button appears in the sign-off, the footer and on the contact page, and the privacy page mentions WhatsApp. Empty hides all of them.

## Your texts and choices: theme.config.ts

`src/config/theme.config.ts` holds:

| Setting | What it holds |
|---------|---------------|
| `language` | The site's language: `'en'`, `'es'`, `'fr'` or `'nl'`. See [Your Site in Your Language](/blog/your-language/). |
| `brandColor` | The brand colour, or `'default'` for the theme's violet. See [Use Your Own Brand Colour](/blog/your-own-brand-colour/). |
| `darkOnly` | `true` for dark mode only. See [Use Dark Mode Only](/blog/dark-mode-only/). |
| `showcase` | The [components page](/components/). Set it to `false` on your own site; you still see it in `pnpm dev`. |
| `home` | The homepage: the browser tab title, the hero's headline and line, the section the droplet lands in, the card it opens into, and the titles above the projects and the posts. |
| `about` | The About page: its hero and facts, the years, a few words on how you work, recent projects and your tools. |
| `pages` | The projects, blog and contact pages: the description for search engines, the line beside the title, the contact page's title, and what a project without a client shows. |
| `signOff` | The sign-off above the footer: its title, text and button labels. |
| `newsletter` | The signup under every post. `show: false` removes it. |
| `footer` | The line beside the copyright. |

In the longer texts, `[text](url)` becomes a link and `**text**` bold.

## Your photo

The About page's hero shows your mark until you give it a photo. Put the photo in `src/assets/about/` and import it at the top of `src/pages/about.astro`; the comment there shows how. A portrait photo stands on the right of the hero on a wide screen.

## After the settings

1. Write your posts in `src/content/blog/<language>/`: `en/` for an English site.
2. Add your projects in `src/content/work/`, with their pictures in `src/assets/work/`.
3. Add yourself as an author in `src/content/authors/`.
4. The menu is in `src/config/nav.config.ts`, the footer's columns in `src/config/footer.config.ts`.
5. Read the privacy page (`src/i18n/privacy/<language>.astro`) and change what doesn't match your site.

Posts and projects have a post of their own: [Writing Posts and Adding Projects](/blog/posts-and-projects/).
