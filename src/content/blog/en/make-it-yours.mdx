Most of what makes Astro Orbit your website is in one file: `src/config/theme.config.ts`. The components read from it, so you don't need to open them to change a name or a line of text.

## What's in it

| Setting | What it holds |
|---------|---------------|
| `demo` | Shows the "About this theme" band above the sign-off. Set it to `false` on your own site. |
| `brandColor` | The brand colour, or `'default'` for the theme's violet. See [Use Your Own Brand Colour](/blog/your-own-brand-colour/). |
| `darkOnly` | `true` for dark mode only, without the light/dark switch. See [Use Dark Mode Only](/blog/dark-mode-only/). |
| `language` | The site's language: `'en'` for English, `'es'` for Spanish, `'fr'` for French, `'nl'` for Dutch. See [Your Site in Your Language](/blog/your-language/). |
| `profile` | Your name, your wordmark (`am/` in the demo), a sentence about you, the site's address, your email, phone and WhatsApp link, where you are, and your social profiles. |
| `home` | The homepage: the browser tab title, the hero, the section the droplet falls into, the card it opens into, and the titles and lines above the projects and the posts. |
| `about` | The About page: its description, the line beside the title, the violet card and the section under it. |
| `testimonials` | The quote cards on the homepage: the title, the line beside it and each quote, with the person's name, their role and the project it's about. Use real quotes, with permission. `show: false` removes the section. |
| `services` | The four stacked cards on the homepage: the title, the line beside it and each card's number, title, icon, tags and text. Use them for your services or for anything else. `show: false` removes the section. |
| `pages` | For the projects, blog and contact pages: the description for search engines and the line beside the title, and the contact page's title. |
| `menu` | The line in the menu. |
| `signOff` | The sign-off above the footer: its title, its text and the labels of its two buttons. |

In the longer texts, `[text](url)` becomes a link and `**text**` becomes bold. Everything else is plain text.

## WhatsApp

Add a WhatsApp link (`https://wa.me/<your number>`) to `profile.whatsapp`, and a WhatsApp button appears in the sign-off, the footer and on the contact page. Leave it empty to hide it.

## After the settings

1. Set `demo` to `false`.
2. Write your posts in `src/content/blog/<language>/`: `en/` for an English site.
3. Add your projects in `src/content/work/`.
4. Add yourself as an author in `src/content/authors/`.
5. Read the privacy page (`src/pages/privacy.astro`) and change what doesn't match your site.

Posts and projects have a post of their own: [Writing Posts and Adding Projects](/blog/posts-and-projects/).
