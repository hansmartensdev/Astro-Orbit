challenge: "My previous website was good, but as a web designer and developer I wanted something more special and attractive: a portfolio, blog and homepage with a wow factor for me and, I think, for visitors too. That comes from the animated 3D hero. A ball comes out of it and follows a track down the homepage until it becomes the first project."
solution: "I wanted a new Astro website, and I chose a new brand colour for it. My previous website was blue. The ball on the homepage is violet, so I made violet the new brand colour. I think the project cards, on the homepage and on the projects page, give the website a professional look. I'm proud of the result so far, and I'm still improving the site, both its design and its development.\n\nSWM26 by [Southwell Media](https://github.com/southwellmedia) used three different fonts: one for the headings, one for the text and one for the small labels. I replaced them with a single font, Geist, so every word on the site is set in the same font."
