challenge: "I wanted a portfolio with a blog in one website: fast, in dark and light mode, and with something a visitor remembers. This website is built with Astro and the Astro Orbit theme.\n\nThe homepage hero has a 3D animation. A droplet comes out of the hero, follows a track down the homepage and turns into a card whose words light up one by one as you scroll. Then the card becomes a droplet again, follows the track further and turns into the first project card. You can see it in the videos on this page."
sections:
  - label: "Colour and fonts"
    text: "I chose violet as the brand colour, because it looks great on the 3D droplet that comes out of the hero. To match that prominent element on the homepage, other elements have the same violet.\n\nThis website uses three fonts, which work together as one style.\n\n**Outfit** is used for the headings and the large titles. It's a geometric sans-serif designed by Rodrigo Fuenzalida. It works well at a large size, and it looks good together with the 3D animation in the hero.\n\n**Archivo** is used for the running text, the buttons and the menu. It's a grotesque sans-serif from Omnibus-Type. It's easy to read, and it pairs well with Outfit.\n\n**IBM Plex Mono** is used for the small labels, such as dates and section names. It's the monospaced member of IBM's Plex family. It fits this combination and style, and it looks good in capitals, which is how the labels are set.\n\nAll three are free, open-source fonts. The website serves them itself, so loading a page doesn't contact a font service such as Google Fonts."
